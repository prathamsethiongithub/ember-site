01 · the oracle

your game crashed.
we know why.

the oracle reads the wreckage — rotated logs, crash reports, the whole causal chain — and names the culprit. when it can't name one, it says so, honestly. no guessing, ever.

  • walks the full Caused by: chain to the root
  • names the mod, or stays silent — never wrong
  • one click to the fix when a fix exists
